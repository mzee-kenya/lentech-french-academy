Pronunciation Tips:
  • 5 (cinq) - The final 'q' is pronounced like a soft 'k'
  • 6 (six) - The 'x' is pronounced like 's' when alone, but silent in combinations
  • 10 (dix) - Similar to 'dees' with a soft 's'
  • 17 (dix-sept) - Pronounced "dee-set" with a silent 'p'
