📖 Grammar: The Verb "S'appeler" (To be called)

The verb s'appeler is a reflexive verb used to tell someone's name.

SubjectConjugationPronunciationMeaning
Jeje m'appelleMy name is
Tutu t'appellesYour name is (informal)
Il/Elle/Onil/elle/on s'appelleHis/her/one's name is
Nousnous nous appelonsOur names are
Vousvous vous appelezYour name is (formal/plural)
Ils/Ellesils/elles s'appellentTheir names are
🎤
Pronunciation Tip: The "ll" in "appelle" is pronounced like a soft "y" sound. "Je m'appelle" sounds like "zhuh mah-pel".

🇫🇷 Cultural Note: Formal vs. Informal Address

In French culture, it's important to use the appropriate level of formality:

  • "Tu" is used with friends, family, children, and people you know well
  • "Vous" is used with strangers, elders, authority figures, and in professional settings
  • When in doubt, start with "vous" – the other person will invite you to use "tu" if appropriate
  • Shaking hands is common in formal introductions; cheek kisses (la bise) are for friends and family
